# Health & Beauty discovery and share colors

Health social artwork uses a soft sage background (`#E8F1EB`). Product text, prices, images, disclosures, positioning and the four export dimensions are unchanged. General product posters retain their existing background. Health membership and enabled status select the palette automatically, including canonical metadata images, downloaded posters and TikTok animation panels/background. Palette-specific versions prevent stale image and video cache reuse; old poster URLs redirect to the current version.

On `/health`, visitors can combine the existing categories/search with price type, availability, currency, price range and sorting. Filters use the existing catalog rules: unknown prices are not invented, and different currencies are not converted. The URL retains selected filters; initial server rendering and Back navigation restore them.

Existing product comparisons additionally show documented ingredients, provider usage and cautions when a health product is selected. Product-specific information, sources, review dates and FAQs continue to use the same offer editor in **Admin → Health → Edit product**. Related health products stay within the department’s category and link to health detail pages.

Manage guides/collections under **Admin → Website content → Guides / Collections**. Connect a health product and mark the page featured to show it on Health & Beauty. Related guides connected through those pages are supported. Featured relevant editorial pages may link to an existing published signup page; these are shown as topic signup choices. The primary department signup is still selected in Health settings. No new contact database, duplicate offer, email sender or consent bypass is added. No medical claims or editorial content are generated.

Changes are local until explicitly deployed. Existing providers may cache social previews after deployment.
